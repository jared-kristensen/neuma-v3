Roll up · Detail

Same page as a Context. Issues, actions and decisions roll up from the linked Contexts, each showing where it came from. Patterns are found across all of them. Contexts replaces Sources. Share and ⋯ work like the Context page. Share opens Manage access with Roll up roles (Editor changes which Contexts are in it and shares, Viewer sees the full Roll up), a note that sharing doesn’t open the Contexts underneath, and Make private. ⋯ has Edit Roll up, Make private, Hide for me and Delete for everyone for the Owner. Switch to a Roll up Jared doesn’t own (Sarah’s or Daniel’s) to see the Viewer header: no Share or Add Context, and ⋯ shows Hide for me only.
