Screen 8

Settings · Your connections

Everyone sees this. It’s how Neuma works bottom up: anyone can connect any tool that works for one person, and Neuma only sees their data. Notetakers and AI tools sit here because people pick them for themselves. Any tool can hold more than one account. Manage or View opens a drawer with that person’s own sync status and meetings, even for company connections. People can remove a meeting they host from Neuma, which deletes the transcript and everything pulled from it. Meetings they only attended can’t be removed here, since others rely on them. Company connections live in Integrations, which only Neuma admins see. When an admin connects a tool for everyone, the row says Connected for everyone at acmegtm.com and the personal connection folds in. Admins also see Admin settings → at the right of the tabs, a shortcut to the same page as the account menu. Members see only the two tabs.
