A People Context is made automatically between two people. Only the two of them can open it. Summary leads with Needs attention, which both people see, like an agenda made ahead of time. Under it is the two-way ledger: what you owe, what they owe, what is open between you. Might be useful is different for each person: it draws on other Contexts that person can open, and only they see it. Meetings and Emails each have a tab. A shared meeting shows only what involves both people. Ask opens a panel that answers as Sarah’s AI. Open What Sarah’s AI can draw on is not decided. This mock uses what both people can open.
