People Context · Sarah M.

A People Context is made automatically between two people. Only the two of them can open it. Summary leads with Needs attention, which both people see, like an agenda made ahead of time. It shows 3 of up to 5, with Show 2 more. Under it, Actions shows each owner’s initials and skips anything already in Needs attention. Issues, Actions and Decisions use the Screen 3 rows. Might be useful is different for each person: it draws on other Contexts that person can open, and only they see it. Each item opens its source and can be dismissed. Meetings has its own tab. Emails feed the items and show as their source; there is no Emails tab. A shared meeting shows only what involves both people. Ask opens a panel that answers as Sarah’s AI. Open What Sarah’s AI can draw on is not decided. This mock uses what both people can open.
