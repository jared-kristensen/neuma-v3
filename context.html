Share opens Manage access: Owner, Editor and Viewer, plus any Roll ups that include it. Make private, in the ⋯ menu or under General access in Share, asks first, then marks the Context Private and removes everyone else. Sharing it again lifts it. Manage access also holds the Full sources switch: off by default, Owner only, and it covers Neuma and AI tools together. Tick View as a Viewer to see the page with the switch off: Share, Add source and the Owner’s menu items go, and on the Sources tab the list stays, each source still opens to what Neuma pulled from it, and the transcript, thread or file itself is locked. Sources have a Status column that says only whether the transcript is there, with the same labels and dots as the Integrations meeting lists: Ready, Waiting on Zoom (or Meet, Teams), No transcript. Every source stays in the Context. Neuma extracts issues, actions and decisions from the latest 10 as they come in, and what it found stays. Open the oldest meeting (Sep 15) to see one added past the 10: its drawer says Nothing extracted. In Everything only, a meeting whose host isn’t on Neuma shows Host not on Neuma, and its drawer has Invite [host]. When every meeting is the same series, rows lead with the date instead of repeating the title. Rows and the source drawer show no per-meeting summary or topics. The drawer shows what Neuma found in that meeting. Remove from Context asks first and says what goes: the items that came only from that meeting. Then the row leaves Sources, with Undo.

Mockup

Context · Said and seen

A mockup, separate from Screen 3, to try one rule. The rule: Neuma records what people committed to, and judges what the problems are and what to do next. Decisions and Actions hold only what people said. Issues are Neuma’s list: each row shows its basis, either who said it and when, or “Identified by Neuma” with how many meetings it was seen across. Patterns sit beside Issues as the evidence. A Neuma-identified issue has Dismiss on hover, which asks why. Summary is the same as Screen 3, shown here as a copy. Suggested actions sit beside the list, each with the pattern it came from, Accept and Dismiss. A count on the tab says how many wait. Accepted items join the list marked Suggested and who accepted them. Dismiss asks why, which trains the model. At most 5 open: 3 show, the rest behind Show more. Each shows its age and retires on its own after 30 days with no new evidence, when its pattern goes away, or when a meeting covers it. A covered item shows “Neuma suggested this”. Past suggestions keeps 30 days of dismissed, retired and covered ones, with Restore for dismissed. Unaccepted suggestions never leave the Context: not into Roll ups, Slack, the Zoom panel or the MCP. Open the Issues tab, then the Actions tab.
